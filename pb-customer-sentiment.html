Customer Sentiment AI

Two-layer sentiment analysis pipeline on 10,000 Amazon reviews. Layer 1: VADER lexicon-based scoring on all reviews (fast, free, no API limit). Layer 2: HuggingFace BART zero-shot classification on 100 negative reviews for deep complaint topic extraction (free tier). Shows knowing when to use rules vs AI — a key senior analyst skill.

Q1 — Sentiment by star rating
Q2 — Monthly negative trend

Does AI sentiment align with star ratings? Validation of VADER output against human ratings.

SELECT score AS star_rating, vader_label, COUNT(*) AS count, ROUND(100.0*COUNT(*)/SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (PARTITION BY score), 1) AS pct FROM reviews_with_sentiment GROUP BY score, vader_label ORDER BY score, count DESC;

Are negative reviews growing or shrinking over time?

SELECT SUBSTR(review_date, 1, 7) AS month, COUNT(*) AS total, SUM(CASE WHEN vader_label IN ('Negative','Very Negative') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS neg_count FROM reviews_with_sentiment GROUP BY month ORDER BY month;
